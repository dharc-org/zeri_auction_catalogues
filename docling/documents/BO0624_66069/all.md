- 146 THE EMPEROR'S CARPET:A Persian carpet,made in a Court Factory of the Safidian dynasty,probably in the timeof ShahTahmasp,1524-1576

The carpet is finely woven with an arabesque design terminating in large formal rosette-like blossoms, while introduceda among the stems of the foliage are birds, animals in combat,mythical beasts,lions, antelopes,&amp;c., the whole executed in colours on a rich ruby ground; the border consists of a similar arabesque design with the scrollwork terminating in animals'heads,in colours on a dark green ground, and witha narrow outer edge of ruby. Dividing the centre of the carpet from the border is a narrow yellow and blue band enclosing poetical inscriptions

The warp and wept are of silk and the pile of wool; dyed in twenty-one colours

25ft.long,10 ft.8 in.wide
